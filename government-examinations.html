NDA & NA

Conducting Body
UPSC
Purpose
Army, Navy, Air Force
Minimum Eligibility
Educational qualification The qualification depends on the service preference: Army Wing of NDA Class 12 pass or equivalent from a recognized board. Physics and Mathematics are not compulsory for the Army Wing. Air Force and Naval Wings of NDA Class 12 pass with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) or the qualifications prescribed by UPSC. Indian Naval Academy Course (10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme) Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Candidates appearing in Class 12 can also apply, subject to the conditions in the relevant notification. Age NDA uses a date-of-birth window rather than a simple " 18–20 years " rule. The exact eligible date-of-birth range is specified separately in each notification.
