Student Skill Development Checklist

  • Read at least one non-textbook book every month.
  • Improve typing and computer skills.
  • Participate in quizzes, Olympiads and competitions.
  • Build a portfolio of projects and achievements.
  • Develop communication skills in English and your native language.
  • Volunteer in community activities.
  • Stay updated with technology and current affairs.
