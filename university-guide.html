Choosing a University

  • Verify accreditation and recognition.
  • Compare curriculum and faculty expertise.
  • Review placement statistics and internship opportunities.
  • Consider campus facilities, location, and fees.
  • Explore scholarship and financial aid options.
  • Always refer to official admission notifications before applying.
