Career Planning Checklist

  • Identify your interests and strengths.
  • Research career options before choosing a stream or course.
  • Understand entrance examinations and eligibility.
  • Develop communication, digital and problem-solving skills.
  • Seek internships, volunteering or project experience.
  • Review career goals every year.
