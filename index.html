11–12

Stream Selection in Class 11 & 12

Make choices based on your genuine strengths and interests
  • Science Stream (PCM / PCB / PCMB):

    PCM opens Engineering (B.Tech), Architecture, NDA, Data Science. PCB opens MBBS, BDS, Nursing, Pharmacy, Biotech. PCMB keeps both technical & life sciences open.

  • Commerce Stream (With or Without Math):

    Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics. Leads to CA (Chartered Accountancy), B.Com (Hons), BBA, Investment Banking, Management.

  • Humanities / Arts Stream:

    History, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, Economics, Literature. Excellent for Law (CLAT), Civil Services (UPSC), Journalism, Design, and Social Work.
